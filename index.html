Our Story

A Taste of the Himalayas.

Himalayan FruitCha is an apple-based fruit-tea venture inspired by the apple-growing heritage of Jammu & Kashmir.


The idea is to explore value-added products using the fruit-growing identity of the region — transforming apple-inspired flavours into a convenient fruit-tea format.


It is a story of local inspiration, innovation and entrepreneurship, with the potential to create opportunities for regional fruit growers.

Founder & CEO

A Young Vision, Rooted in the Himalayas.

Harsh Rachyal

Founder & CEO — Himalayan FruitCha

📍 Village Kalsian, Nowshera, District Rajouri, Jammu & Kashmir, India


Meet Harsh Rachyal, the young entrepreneur behind Himalayan FruitCha.


Coming from Village Kalsian in Nowshera, District Rajouri, Jammu & Kashmir, Harsh is building a fruit-led tea venture inspired by the apple-growing heritage of his region.


His vision is to introduce a distinctive apple fruit-tea experience, build a brand representing Jammu & Kashmir and explore opportunities for local fruit growers and communities.


His ambition goes beyond creating a tea product. It is about combining innovation, entrepreneurship, regional identity and local economic opportunities.
